`kestrel` extends `m5_challenger`'s own technique: the upgraded M5 (`counter_strategies.py`) adds one bounded, evidence-gated semi-bluff to Talon (`m5_counterplay`) — bet when checked to on the turn with equity below 0.375, but only while a per-half trial/fold tracker shows the bluff is still getting folded to. Kestrel applies the identical, already-validated mechanism to the river as well, with its own independent per-street trial/fold counter (gate: 12 trials, then require a >=25% fold rate), rather than inventing a new adaptation rule. Before settling on this, two other approaches were screened against `m5_challenger` and rejected on evidence: loosening Talon's postflop fold-defense margin (0.30) made results monotonically worse (seed sweeps from -1.10 at margin 0.06 up to the 0.30 baseline), and tightening it beyond 0.30 (0.35-0.50) was flat/noisy with no improvement — so Talon's existing fold threshold was left untouched. Kestrel's full-zoo gate (`league/run.py --bot kestrel --zoo all --seeds 3`, 126 matches) had 0 errors/fallbacks: 106 wins and 20 chops, 0 losses. It beats every other research candidate with a significant margin (`m5_classic` +1.0 to +1.27 bb/deal, `m7_cherki` +0.90 to +1.17, `m1_reference` +2.2 to +2.8, `m1_team_tuned` +1.5 to +1.8, `m11_chosen`/`rook_fast` +0.3 to +0.58, `m5_counterplay` +0.25 to +0.39, `rangefinder` win on 2 of 3 seeds) and is a statistical tie (chop) against the rest of the turn/river pressure fleet (`turnblade`, `duskfox`, `nightjar`, `tidehunter`, `mirrorfin`). Against `m5_challenger` specifically — the bot it was built to beat — a focused 15-seed x 1,000-deal confirmatory run (seeds 2000-2014, recorded in `results.csv`) pooled to +0.0215 bb/deal with an approximate 95% CI of [-0.022, +0.065]: 2 win / 1 loss / 12 chop per-seed, not a statistically significant edge. Kestrel is therefore kept as a research candidate, not promoted; `bots/active.py` still defaults to `m5_challenger`. Timing is safely inside budget: ~2.2-2.5s think time per half of the 8s bank, p99 decision latency under 2ms.
